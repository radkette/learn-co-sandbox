About

What is Al‑Anon?

Al-Anon is…

  • For families and friends of alcoholics.
  • A separate fellowship from Alcoholics Anonymous (AA).
  • Based on the Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions adapted from AA.
  • Compatible with professional treatment.

Local Organization

San Diego Al‑Anon Information Service (AIS or Intergroup) is a regional service arm established and maintained by several districts.

Al‑Anon helps families and friends of alcoholics recover from the effects of living with the problem drinking of a relative or friend. Similarly, Alateen is our recovery program for young people. Alateen groups are sponsored by Al‑Anon members.

Our program of recovery is adapted from Alcoholics Anonymous and is based upon the Twelve Steps, Twelve Traditions, and Twelve Concepts of Service.

The only requirement for membership is that there be a problem of alcoholism in a relative or friend. Whether the alcoholic is still drinking or not, Al‑Anon/Alateen offers hope and recovery to all people affected by the alcoholism of a loved one or friend.

Al‑Anon/Alateen is not affiliated with any other organization or outside entity.

How it works

One day at a time.

There is no magic formula that enables you to help someone stop—or cut back—on their drinking. But Al‑Anon can help you learn how to cope with the challenges of someone else's drinking.

Al‑Anon simplifies a complex problem by suggesting a "One Day at a Time" approach. At every meeting, you can hear people explain how Al‑Anon worked for them.

Al‑Anon members come to understand problem drinking as a family illness that affects everyone in the family. Research shows that problem drinkers' chances for recovery improve when supported by family members in a program like Al‑Anon.

In Al‑Anon/Alateen, members do not give direction or advice. Instead, they share their personal experiences and invite others to "take what they like and leave the rest." The best place to learn how Al‑Anon/Alateen works is at a meeting in your local community. Personal contact is an important element in the healing process.
